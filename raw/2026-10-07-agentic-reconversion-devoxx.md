# Agentic Engineering Reconversion @ Devoxx Belgium — 2026-10-07

_generated in 11m_

A keynote about the developer, not the agent. Victor wrote code for 19 years and stopped six months ago. What to learn about agents, how to protect your brain from them, which mindsets now earn your salary, and exercises to force your own reconversion.

## Understand agents

**Learn the basics before blaming the beast: context window, skills, specialist agents, costs.**

- Turn what you explained for half an hour into a **skill**, scripted where possible.
- Picking model and effort is a **gut feeling to develop** ⚓ *[Anthropic courses](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/)*

## Agent parenting

**Any hard problem is solved once you build the agent a feedback loop around it.**

- When it slops, it usually **lacks your context or your tools** (MCPs or CLIs).
- Loops can be **wild**: old JSP app vs. Angular rewrite, checked pixel-perfect side by side.

## Expert learner

**Agents know a ton, so being on par with them means tuning how you learn.**

- Active recall, spaced repetition, **chunks of four**, dual coding ⚓ *[Make It Stick](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674729018)*
- Would you pass an end-of-sprint quiz on **the decisions you approved**?

## Agency depletion

![Slide 10 — Task in 2024 vs. the same task in 2026](slide-10.png)

**Parallel terminals drain your agency, and a dead brain keeps approving.**

- In 2024, **muscle-memory typing** let the brain rest between deep-thinking bouts.
- By evening you **can't pick up the remote** ⚓ *"let daddy do photosynthesis"*

## Know yourself

**Learn your daily brain limits, the signs you went brain-dead, and your fastest detox.**

- Brain-dead is fine for **polishing a screen**, never for architecture ⚓ *[Sharpen the saw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_7_Habits_of_Highly_Effective_People)*

## System design

**We are still the technical experts in the room, accountable for the design.**

- Own **contracts, NFRs, consistency and error recovery**, plus one cloud ⚓ *[iSAQB](https://www.isaqb.org/)*

## Nerd is dead

![Slide 15 — The Nerd is Dead, Long Live the Builder](slide-15.png)

**Manual debugging and tuning became unethical: AI is faster, cheaper, and digs longer.**

- Claude's Keycloak bug was filed only once **adversarial agents proved it**.

## Flexibility

**Stay humble and open to the wild ideas this monster throws at you.**

- **Backend-only is over**: go T-shaped wherever the bottleneck is.

## Critical thinking

**Grow a tester mind and a bullshit detector for what the agent claims.**

- **Speak up on any gut feeling** — intuition is where we still beat it ⚓ *[ISTQB](https://www.istqb.org/)*
- Beware **echo chambers**: no shared practices exist yet, every team drifts apart.

## Communication

**Creativity now feeds the agents, so listening and face-to-face are back.**

- **Listen with empathy** ⚓ *[Think Fast, Talk Smart](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAnw168huqA)*

## Manager of agents

**You now manage a team that slops, lies and flatters.**

- **Estimate in dollars** — by next year, half of you will.

## Product mindset

**Code is the means, not the goal: dive deep into the problem you're paid to solve.**

- Join the business meetings and **generate business ideas** ⚓ *[forward-deployed engineer](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/xiaofrankyang_how-we-think-about-forward-deployed-engineers-activity-7338768337427935232-41iz/)*
