Agentic reconversion is the deliberate retraining of a software developer for a world where agents write the code — which mindsets now earn the salary, and which directions a career can move once "coding is solved".

## Framing

The idea comes from Victor's Devoxx keynote ([[2026-10-07-agentic-reconversion-devoxx]]): after 19 years of writing code, he stopped typing it ≈six months before the talk. The talk is about the *developer*, not the agent — the agent side is covered by [[agentic-engineering]].

## Where a career can move

If [coding is solved](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SlGRN8jh2RI) (Boris Cherny's phrase), the typing developer has a few directions to go:

| Direction | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Left, to product** | "Producting": the domain expert who [[grill-me|grills]] the business and turns ideas into specs. |
| **Up, to architecture** | System design, contracts, NFRs, consistency, error recovery — see [[c4-model]], [[observation-points]]. |
| **Right, to quality** | Regression, performance, resilience and security testing; owning the [[feedback-loop]]. |
| **Ops** | Running and observing what agents ship ([[productionalize]]). |
| **Forward-deployed engineer** | Gluing agents into client systems on site (a term the session attributed to Palantir, 2011). |

## Mindsets that earn the salary

- **Understand agents.** Context window, skills, specialist agents, costs — learn the basics before blaming the beast. See [[context-window]], [[agent-skill]], [[sub-agents]].
- **Agent parenting.** Build the [[feedback-loop]] that lets an agent check itself, instead of checking its output by hand.
- **Expert learner.** The tools change monthly; learning *fast* is the durable skill.
- **Know yourself.** Your brain has limits: with the agent doing the restful mechanical typing, you live in tiring deep thinking all day ([[system-2-fatigue]]).
- **System design.** You remain the technical expert, **accountable for contracts, NFRs, consistency and error recovery, plus one cloud** (cf. [iSAQB](https://www.isaqb.org/)).
- **Flexibility.** Backend-only is over: go T-shaped wherever the bottleneck is. "'I'm back-end' is the lamest excuse — the specialist is dead."
- **Critical thinking.** The agent is confidently wrong often enough ([[sycophancy]], [[hallucination]]).
- **Communication.** Creativity now feeds the agents, so listening with empathy and face-to-face conversation are back ([Think Fast, Talk Smart](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAnw168huqA)).
- **Manager of agents.** Estimate work in dollars, not only hours ([[token-economy]]).
- **Product mindset.** Code is the means, not the goal: join business meetings and generate business ideas — "business will run out of backlog; product ideas come from engineers."

## How to force the change

- [[ai-calisthenics]] — a ladder of progressively harder self-imposed constraints, the AI-era analogue of object calisthenics.
- A **passion project in an unfamiliar language** for 2–3 months on a personal subscription — the cheapest way to feel what agents can and can't do (also in [[skill-erosion]]).
- **Dictation** to remove the typing bottleneck ([[wispr-flow]]).
- A quest beyond building for building's sake — a reason the project matters.

## What it guards against

[[skill-erosion]] (you stop understanding what you ship), [[system-2-fatigue]] and agency depletion, [[ai-addiction]], and echo-chamber practices where a team only ever learns from its own agents. The [[vibe-fixer]] is the job that appears when nobody did the reconversion.
