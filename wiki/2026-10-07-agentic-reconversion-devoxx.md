Victor Rentea's Devoxx Belgium keynote about the developer rather than the agent: after 19 years of writing code he stopped typing it six months earlier, and the talk asks what to learn about agents, how to protect your brain from them, which mindsets now earn the salary, and which exercises force your own reconversion. Its spine: any hard problem yields once the agent has a feedback loop; parallel terminals deplete your agency and a dead brain keeps approving; manual debugging and tuning are now unethical; code is the means, not the goal — so shift left to product, up to architecture, or right to quality; your fingers are the bottleneck, so dictate; and a quest of helping others keeps you sane.
