Two hands-on hours with a bank's developers using IBM Bob as the harness, on real tasks against the petclinic repo: what fills the context before your first prompt (≈9k tokens of vendor prompt, tools, skills and rules), why caches are per model and exact-prefix, driving a browser and the issue tracker from the agent (Playwright CLI scraping a tracker with no MCP), turning a session's discoveries into skills and glob-scoped rules shared through a team git repo, grilling a vague ticket with the business before any code, keeping a code-mined LLM wiki in sync by cron, and asking for an adversarial sub-agent before any human review. Its fact-check section corrects the wiki's long-context recall figure.
