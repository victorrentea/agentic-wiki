One-day hands-on workshop at a large bank, driving agents mostly through GitHub Copilot CLI (some Claude Code) from inside a locked-down corporate VM, on the open-source petclinic project. The spine of the day: stop treating the agent as a chatbot and treat it as a managed engineer — give it feedback loops ("humans should not check the work of AI, tools should"), give it your already-authenticated CLIs instead of your keys, burn every failure into AGENTS.md or a skill, grill vague tickets down a decision tree, mob-review the spec, and never approve what you can't explain. It also covered harness ranking for corporate Copilot users, the Explain/Find/Do cost modes, architecture-as-text (C4/PlantUML) edited by prompt, a SessionStart hook that injects your assigned tickets, and red-teaming an access grant with the model itself.
